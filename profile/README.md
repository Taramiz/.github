### What Taramiz does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Crypto in** | Give every customer their own deposit address for USDT, USDC and major coins across popular networks. |
| **Convert** | Turn stablecoins and crypto into Naira and other currencies at a clear, locked rate. |
| **Pay out** | Send funds automatically to your bank account, or to your end users' verified bank accounts. |
| **Wallets** | Hold balances for your business and for each of your users, with every movement on a double-entry ledger. |
| **Notify** | Get signed webhooks and real-time updates the moment money is confirmed. |

### How we build

- **Verified before credited.** We only mark money as received once we've confirmed it ourselves. No early "paid" that turns out wrong later.
- **Every naira accounted for.** Every transaction balances on our ledger, and every balance can be rebuilt from its entries.
- **Once and only once.** Payouts are idempotent end to end, so a retry never sends money twice.
- **Built for developers.** Clean REST API, idempotency keys, signed webhooks, sandbox keys and SDKs.

### Who it's for

Exchanges, remittance apps, online stores, marketplaces and fintechs that want to accept crypto and settle in local currency without stitching together wallets, exchanges and bank transfers by hand.
